How it works

From photo to diagnosis

1

Upload

A leaf photo is resized to 224×224 and normalised to ImageNet statistics.

2

Classify

EfficientNet-B0 scores all 38 classes; softmax probabilities rank the top 3.

3

Explain

Grad-CAM back-propagates from the winning class to heat-map the leaf region behind the decision.

Grad-CAM explainability

The demo returns three views side by side: the original leaf, a whitened heatmap, and a jet-colour overlay — so every prediction is auditable, not a black box.

Under the hood

Model & training

Architecture

  • EfficientNet-B0, ImageNet-pretrained, classifier head swapped for a 38-way layer.
  • AdamW (lr 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4) + ReduceLROnPlateau.
  • 10 epochs, fp16 mixed precision, best-checkpoint selection.
  • Augmentation: flips, rotation, colour jitter.

Data & integrity

  • PlantVillage: 54,304 leaf photos, 38 classes, 14 crops.
  • Held-out official test split + 10% stratified validation.
  • Leakage audit: no leaf id spans both training and test.
  • Confusion matrix + classification report in the notebook.
